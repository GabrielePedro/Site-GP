International signup: fewer barriers to completing registration

Simplifying signup to reduce abandonment and support the platform’s expansion into the European market.

Problem

Excessive requirements, confusing language, and concerns about sharing personal data made signup harder to complete.

My contribution

I led discovery with 128 responses and four interviews, redesigned the flow, and revised the communication with the Content Designer.

Decision

I moved requirements to the post-signup stage, prioritizing initial completion and deferring profile completeness.

What I would do differently today

I would align instrumentation before launch and track completion of the deferred steps and subsequent engagement.

Timeline
May–Jun 2025.
Role
Product Designer
Scope
Quantitative and qualitative research, funnel analysis, heuristics, UX/UI and handoff, AI applied to design, and product analytics.
Status
Implemented in production.
