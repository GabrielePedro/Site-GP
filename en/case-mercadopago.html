Balance holds: clarity and autonomy to settle debts

Redesigning the balance hold flow to explain restrictions and make it easier to settle debts.

Context

Problem: Full holds prevented people from using their money, made it harder to repay the debt itself, and undermined trust in the platform.

My contribution: I analyzed complaints, mapped the journey, and designed alternative flows and communication.

Decision: I prioritized late payments, proposing partial holds and the use of available funds to repay debt; fraud scenarios remained outside the redesign.

Result: The copy comparison presented records payment within three hours increasing from 19% to 25% and understanding of the hold from 47% to 68%.

What I would do differently today: I would validate rule changes with Risk and Compliance before developing the interface in detail.
