Balance holds

clarity and autonomy to

settle debts!

Problem

In the digital account at Mercado Pago, holding the entire balance made it harder to repay the debt itself. The lack of explanations and timelines undermined trust and increased reliance on support.

Role

I analyzed complaints, mapped the journey, and designed an alternative flow and communication.

Scope

Documentary research, heuristic analysis, business rules, flows, UX writing and UI, A/B testing.

Decision

I prioritized late payments, proposing partial holds and the use of available funds to repay debt; fraud scenarios remained outside the redesign.

What I would do differently today

I would validate rule changes with Risk and Compliance before developing the interface in detail.

Status

Project with documented results.

Timeline

Aug–Oct 2024

Results

The new balance hold policy, combined with clearer communication and UX writing, had a direct impact on the business and the user experience:

  • –18% support requests

    Related to held balances, reducing support operating costs.

  • +21pp in understanding

    Of deadlines and penalties.

  • +6pp in overdue payment rate

    After the first notification (within 3 hours).

  • +9pp in perceived clarity

    In how the rules are communicated.

These results validated the strategy of making rules more proportional and transparent, reducing friction while maintaining security and compliance.
