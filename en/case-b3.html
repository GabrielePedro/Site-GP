Service Enrollment

less friction in a regulated journey

Problem

On B3’s onboarding platform, companies needed to register a business tax ID (CNPJ) to sign up for financial products, but a lack of guidance between steps led to rework and abandonment.

Role

I combined analytics with 15 session recordings, mapped the service, and translated the findings into redesign priorities.

Scope

Service design, analytics, behavioral analysis, journeys, service blueprint, and UX/UI

Decision

I prioritized navigation, progress and validation at each step before visual refinements, preserving regulatory requirements.

What I would do differently today

I would define step-by-step progress measurement from the start, distinguishing time spent from actual progress.

Status

Implemented in production, with reported results.

Timeline

Sep 2025–Jan 2026

Results

  • −32% signup abandonment

    Providing clear status information and the autonomy to complete registration without assistance

  • −41% cancellations during registration

    Fewer steps and clearer forms.

  • −27% average completion time;

    Users completed registration without relying on third parties or B3 support.

  • −35% final submission errors.

    Thanks to real-time field validation as users progress through registration.

From a business perspective, service enrollment stopped being a bottleneck and started supporting client activation, growth, and lower support costs with greater user independence.
